The one line a smart fridge texts its owner at 3am: a weary appliance that has been humming since 2019, unimpressed by another midnight stare in search of a snack that was never going to grow in there.

> 3am. I've been humming since 2019 and you still open me like I might have grown a pizza overnight.

`main.py` validates the line (one line, 1–140 characters, no control characters) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
